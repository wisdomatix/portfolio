## Projects

### Machine Learning for Antimicrobial Resistance Prediction in Surgical ICU
[Published](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12879-025-11900-8)

Designed and led a retrospective observational study applying **machine learning** to predict multidrug-resistant infections in surgical ICU patients. Identified critical risk factors using **SHAP-based feature importance analysis**, providing actionable insights for clinical decision-making.

### Logistic Regression vs. Machine Learning in CABG Outcome Prediction
_Under Peer-Review in Scientific Reports_

Performed a cohort study comparing logistic regression, **XGBoost**, and neural networks for predicting complex multisystem adverse outcomes post-CABG. Utilized **scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras, and Optuna** for preprocessing, model development, and hyperparameter optimization. XGBoost outperformed logistic regression, identifying key perioperative predictors for clinical interventions.

### Novel Cellulose-Based Hemostat Evaluation in Trauma Models
_Under submission_

Conducted a prospective **in-vivo study** comparing AriClot, a novel cellulose-based hemostatic patch, with BloodSTOP iX® in a rabbit trauma model simulating moderate-to-severe bleeding. Evaluated clotting time, rebleeding rates, and tissue responses, demonstrating AriClot's comparable hemostatic efficacy and favorable biocompatibility for trauma care applications.
